# ReelCraft Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

ReelCraft is a video editor that works entirely on your phone.

## What we collect

Nothing. ReelCraft has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

## Your videos and music

- ReelCraft only opens the videos and music files you choose with the phone's picker.
- All editing, sound analysis and exporting happens on your phone. Your files are never uploaded.
- Exported videos are stored in the app's temporary storage until you share them or save them to your gallery. The app deletes the previous export when you make a new one.

## Permissions

On Android 8 and 9 only, ReelCraft asks for storage access the first time you tap Save to gallery, so it can put the video in your Camera folder. Newer Android versions need no permission.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, Android shows you a list of apps. The video goes only to the app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy.

## Changes

If a future version adds optional online features, this policy will be updated before release and those features will be clearly marked and off by default.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue on the ReelCraft GitHub page.
